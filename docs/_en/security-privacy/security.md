# Security

## Principles

- Least privilege.
- Tenant separation.
- Strong authentication for privileged roles.
- Granular, verifiable authorisation.
- Encryption in transit and protection at rest.
- Secure handling of credentials and secrets.
- Audit of accesses and sensitive actions.
- Verifiable backup and restore.
- Vulnerability management and updates.

## Sensitive operations

Provisioning, wipe, lock, data export, global policy changes and access to cyberbullying cases require strengthened controls, a reason and logging.

## Threat modelling

The design must consider at least: compromised teacher/parent accounts, administrator abuse, cross-tenant access, device theft, abuse of the reporting channel, data exfiltration, unavailable Android provider and misconfiguration.

## Incident response

Define roles, classification, containment, communication, recovery, evidence preservation and applicable notification duties (including GDPR breach notification).
