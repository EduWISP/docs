# Cyberbullying & Digital Wellbeing

## Purpose

The module supports prevention, reporting and responsible handling of cyberbullying incidents and promotes digital wellbeing. It is not a student surveillance system and does not replace school procedures, support services or the competent authorities.

## Features

### 1. Reporting
- Accessible, understandable interface.
- Ability to ask for help or report an incident.
- Distinction between support request, report and urgent situation.
- Clear information about who will receive the report and what happens next.
- Reports not exposed to unauthorised parties; any anonymity must be designed carefully so as not to promise absolute confidentiality when it cannot be guaranteed.

### 2. Case handling
- Case ID.
- Date, category and voluntarily provided description.
- Authorised contact person and case status.
- Log of actions and accesses.
- Follow-up and justified closure.
- Escalation and immediate protection procedures, to be defined with schools.

### 3. Prevention
- Digital education materials.
- Paths for students, teachers and families.
- Information on how to ask for help.
- School campaigns and initiatives.

### 4. Digital wellbeing
- Aggregated, contextualised usage indicators, only if necessary and proportionate.
- Reminders and educational content.
- No individual student rankings.
- No reputational or predictive scores.

## What it must not do

- Read chats, emails or private messages indiscriminately.
- Acquire photos, audio or personal files without a specific legal basis and procedure.
- Use keylogging or covert screen capture.
- Track location continuously.
- Automatically classify a student as victim or perpetrator.
- Generate automatic sanctions or disciplinary decisions.

## Assisted analysis

Any future automated support, if ever assessed, should only help authorised staff organise voluntarily reported information. It falls under the EU AI Act, must be legally and technically reviewed, explainable, subject to human oversight and must not produce automated decisions affecting students.

## Legal references

The module must support, without replacing, the procedures of Italian Law 71/2017 as amended by Law 70/2024, including the school contact person role. See the [legal framework](../security-privacy/legal-framework.md).

## Case governance

Before designing the UI we must define:
- who can receive and view a report;
- who the school contact person is;
- how to handle conflicts of interest;
- how to inform student and family;
- retention and deletion periods;
- procedure for emergencies and immediate risk;
- data export and access;
- audit and handling of access abuse.

## Data protection

Reports may contain highly sensitive data. A specific assessment of purpose, legal basis, privacy roles, minimisation, access, retention, security and DPIA is required before any implementation.
