# Mobile Device Management

## Device lifecycle

1. Procurement and administrative registration.
2. Provisioning through an officially supported method.
3. Association with school, site and class.
4. Assignment to a student or group.
5. Application of baselines and policies.
6. Monitoring of the minimum necessary technical state.
7. Reassignment, maintenance or decommissioning.

## Candidate features

- Essential hardware/software inventory.
- Enrollment status and last sync.
- App catalogue.
- Mandatory and optional apps.
- Managed configurations.
- Android restrictions.
- Kiosk or multi-app mode, if supported.
- Wi-Fi and certificates, if supported.
- Updates and maintenance.
- Remote lock and wipe with strengthened authorisation and confirmation.

## Safeguards

High-impact actions (wipe, lock, unassignment) require dedicated permissions, explicit confirmation, a reason and audit. The inventory must avoid collecting unnecessary personal data.
