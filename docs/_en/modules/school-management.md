# School management

## Organisational model

EduWISP must represent:
- organisation/school;
- sites;
- classes and teaching groups;
- teachers and authorised staff;
- students;
- parents and guardians;
- devices and assignments.

## Proposed roles

| Role | Scope |
|---|---|
| Platform Administrator | Global technical management, with limited and logged data access |
| School Administrator | School configuration |
| IT Administrator | Devices, apps and technical policies |
| Head teacher / delegate | Oversight within assigned powers |
| Teacher | Authorised teaching functions |
| Cyberbullying contact person | Access to assigned cases and procedures |
| Student | Viewing information and reporting |
| Parent/guardian | Authorised family functions |

Roles are a proposal: actual delegations must follow school organisation and applicable law.

## Teaching modes

Profiles for lessons, labs, tests and out-of-school activities are to be assessed. Temporary modes must define duration, who activates them, scope, rationale and behaviour when connectivity is lost.
