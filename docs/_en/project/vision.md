# Vision, goals and scope

## Vision

Build an open platform that helps schools manage educational devices with centralised tools, understandable rules and protection of students.

## Goals

1. Centrally manage school-owned tablets.
2. Distribute educational apps and configurations.
3. Organise devices by school, site, class and student.
4. Coordinate school and family policies.
5. Support prevention and handling of cyberbullying reports.
6. Guarantee portability, documentation and self-hosting.
7. Minimise the personal data processed.

## Initial scope

- School-owned Android tablets, with or without Google services (ADR-0003).
- School-owned devices.
- Fully Managed through an open-source EduWISP DPC.
- Only open-source, free components (ADR-0009).
- Debian GNU/Linux 13 Trixie servers.
- Multi-tenancy for multiple schools.

## Out of initial scope

- BYOD as a primary scenario.
- General surveillance of private life.
- Replacing emergency services, social services or official school procedures.
- Automated disciplinary decisions.
