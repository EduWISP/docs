Privacy and compliance

Approach

EduWISP must follow privacy by design and by default. A device being owned by the school does not remove the student’s rights and expectations of privacy. Applicable laws are listed in the legal framework; compliance is a blocking constraint (ADR-0008).

Topics to assess

  • Privacy roles of school, vendor and hosting provider.
  • Purpose and legal basis for each processing activity.
  • Notices understandable by students and families.
  • Data minimisation and purpose limitation.
  • Retention and deletion.
  • Data subject rights.
  • Transfers and data location.
  • Processors and contracts.
  • Security and breach management.
  • DPIA.

Cyberbullying data

Reports must be processed in a separate domain, with restricted access and defined retention. No more information than needed for case handling may be collected.

Note

This documentation is not legal advice. Before processing real data, a DPIA and an assessment with the DPO and qualified advisers are required, considering the GDPR, Italian law and supervisory authority guidance.
